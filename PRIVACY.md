# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: June 11, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to the **Mystic Beasts Evolution** mobile application (the "App") developed by **Samjo**.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**Mystic Beasts Evolution** does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data, user identifiers, geolocation, or device telemetry. 

* **Local Storage Only**: All game progress, save states, settings, and milestones are stored locally on your device (using standard web `localStorage` within the app wrapper).
* **No Network Transmissions**: The game runs entirely client-side on your device. No data is sent to external servers or third-party servers.
* **No Tracking**: We do not use cookies, tracking identifiers, third-party analytics packages, or advertising networks.

## 2. Children's Privacy
Because we do not collect any personal information, the App complies with children's online privacy protection standards worldwide (such as COPPA and GDPR). It is 100% safe for players of all ages.

## 3. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
